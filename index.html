Research

HONOURS THESIS
JAN–APR 2026

Hybrid reinforcement learning for autonomous cyber defence

I built an end-to-end system in PyTorch for CybORG Cage Challenge 2. A frozen cybersecurity LLM (Cyber-Risk-Llama-3-8B) acts as a teacher to a PPO agent with an Intrinsic Curiosity Module, through action masking and an auxiliary teacher-alignment loss.

The hybrid did not beat the PPO-ICM baseline. Its teacher repeated a few recommendations instead of responding to the network state, which biased early training and helps explain the instability. This was a 300-episode run limited by compute, so it is a study of what went wrong, not a final verdict on the method.

Supervised by Dr. Srinivas Sampalli, Dr. Jaume Manero and Dr. Darshana Upadhyay

NSERC USRA
MAY–AUG 2025
FOLLOW-UP 2026

Do ML intrusion detectors work on networks they weren't trained on?

With an NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Award, I replicated Cantone et al. (IEEE Access, 2024). In 2026 I rebuilt it independently on four datasets with over 34 million network flows, matching 77 of the paper's top 78 selected features.

The result: validating on the same dataset can't pick a model that transfers. Near-identical models scored anywhere from 0.03 to 0.48 MCC on another network, and duplicated flows, 23–27% of the 2018 data, inflated one model's score from 0.15 to 0.34.

Supervised by Dr. Nur Zincir-Heywood and Dr. Adetokunbo Makanju
